Describe a need-validation task

Good tasks to send: a need you want to validate (does it hold, and where is the weakest link), a review that needs evidence behind it (MRD/PRD/BRD), or an evidence-grading workshop with your team. State the question first; we agree on scope after.

Scope and feeSCOPE AND FEE, PER TASK

No standard price list is published and there are no fixed packages. After reading the task description I first decide whether to take it: if it fits, you get a written quote covering scope, timeline, and fee, and work starts only once both sides confirm. Conclusions are stated within their evidence, and semantic review and product sign-off stay on your side — the same discipline the open-source engine follows.

Typical delivery shape: a seven-node reasoning chain plus an evidence ledger (E0–E5), an audit report, MRD/PRD/BRD projections, and one review of the conclusions; or a half-day/one-day evidence-grading workshop run on your team's real questions.
