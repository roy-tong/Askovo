A general agent can reason. Askovo brings real evidence into the study.RESEARCH BEYOND THE PROMPT

A polished research answer is only as useful as the material behind it. Askovo helps a team go beyond the information already in the prompt.

Collect real materialGather community feedback, interview material, competitor records and authorized exports with source, time and failure history.
Test demand assumptionsKeep concept feedback, prototype responses, willingness-to-pay statements and observed behavior distinct; simulated users do not become demand evidence.
Move and revisit the decisionKeep support, counterexamples and unknowns attached to each claim, then revisit the same decision as evidence changes.

From research execution to demand validation, with every step tied to source materialFROM EVIDENCE TO VALIDATION

1 · Frame the decisionStart with a costly product choice, its scope and the assumptions that could falsify it.
2 · Collect and importUse community feedback, interview material, competitor records, authorized exports and concept or prototype feedback.
3 · Analyze with provenanceQuantitative and semantic analysis stay linked to exact quotes, counterexamples and coverage gaps.
4 · Validate and decideKeep stated interest, willingness to pay and observed behavior distinct; the decision owner chooses and revisits the next move.

The shared research chain remains scene → user → pain → need → feature → product → business. Its value depends on real material and sound validation, not on generating a more confident report.
