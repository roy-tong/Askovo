From data to product definition, one reasoned step at a timeFROM DATA TO DEFINITION

1 · Collect automaticallyGather user, community and market data around the product question so research starts with real material.
2 · Analyze quantitativelyStudy distributions, frequencies, differences and counterexamples to see which patterns deserve attention.
3 · Build the definitionReason step by step through scene, user, pain, need, feature, product and business.
4 · Decide and revisitTurn the judgment into a Decision Brief and action plan, then refine it as new data arrives.

Askovo’s goal is not to generate a polished answer for you. It is to make data-driven product definition a capability every team can practice.
