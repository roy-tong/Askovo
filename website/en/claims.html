CLAIMS & BOUNDARIES

What we claim, and what we do not claim

Askovo's value lies in knowing how far the evidence reaches. This page puts the product's own claims under the same discipline: what can be claimed is stated plainly, and what must not be passed off as more is said outright.

Six conclusion types must not be conflatedSIX CONCLUSION TYPES

TypeAnswersMust not substitute for
needWho has what unmet job in what sceneFeature requests, single cases, prevalence
acceptanceWhether users understand or accept the solutionLiking ≠ will use / will buy
usabilityWhether the task can be completed; learnable and low-errorUsable ≠ demand established
performanceWhether it meets the bar under stated conditionsBenchmark score ≠ experience
commercialPurchase, retention, repurchase, returnsStated intent ≠ transaction
safetyUsage risk and compliance gapsUser trials ≠ certification

E0–E5 grade demand and commercial evidence strength only — they are not a product/commercial reasoning score; usability, engineering performance, safety, and feasibility each have their own verification requirements.

What the tests and the sample proveWHAT TESTS PROVE

EvidenceCan claimMust not pose as
Unit-test matrix (Python 3.9–3.12; current size in CI)Command behavior, structure validation, and gate logic match the specThat real demand holds, product quality, market size
Offline synthetic sample (askovo demo)The chain, evidence references, and audit logic are self-consistentAny real user or commercial fact
CI passing (tests.yml)Those behaviors are reproducible on supported versionsRelease feasibility, compliance, or certification
Collection via the official GitHub/Reddit APIsRaw records from authorized sources, with bias recordedA sample representing a population; record count = user count

Inferences we never makeNEVER

  • No purchase intent treated as a real purchase, no adoption rate treated as a product outcome, no one-time liking treated as repurchase
  • No convenience sample extrapolated to prevalence or market size; a market estimate must state population, boundary, and source in full, and null means unknown, not zero
  • No decided (a record of the current choice) passed off as release approval, commercial proof, or causal effect — delivery, outcome, and causality are three independent states
  • No unauthorized scraping; blocked connectors, login walls, and platform challenges are never bypassed
  • All automated deliverables are drafts by default; a passing CLI run never replaces human semantic review, product sign-off, or risk acceptance

The full boundaries are in the repository's README boundaries section and core concepts.
